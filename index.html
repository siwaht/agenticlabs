Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At Flotlabs, protecting your company's data and your customers' personal information is our highest priority. This policy outlines how we collect, process, and secure data when providing enterprise AI solutions.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We process data solely for the purpose of operating and improving the custom AI agents deployed for your business. This includes chat logs, voice transcripts, and user interaction metadata. We do not sell this data to third parties.

3. Model Training & Isolation

Unlike public foundational models, the proprietary models we build for your enterprise are trained exclusively on your provided data. Interaction data is never pooled or used to train models for other clients.

4. Security Standards

We adhere to SOC 2 Type II compliance standards. All data in transit and at rest is encrypted using AES-256. Database instances are isolated per client within private VPCs.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Interaction logs are retained according to your specific organizational requirements (default is 90 days). You maintain the right to instantly purge any stored user data upon request.
